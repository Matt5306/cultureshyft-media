# CultureShyft Media

You deserve media that makes your message clear and gives people a reason to care. That is the work I want CultureShyft Media to do.

I’m Mark Andrew, a Cultural Media Artifact Engineer in the Detroit area, Michigan. I make flyers, social posts, campaign sets, logos, websites, and rebrands. Each piece starts with a question: what should someone understand, feel, or do when they see this?

The Y in CultureShyft gives that idea a shape. For me, it suggests a change in direction. A familiar message can take a different path. A small business can show up with more confidence. A community program can make its invitation easier to understand. The spelling gives the name a distinct look and reminds me to make deliberate choices.

Moving people can mean bringing someone through a door. It can mean helping a parent find the right program, giving a customer a clear next step, or making an event easy to share. Sometimes it starts with a feeling: this is for me.

I make this work for small businesses, churches, schools, youth programs, creators, and community organizations. You bring the purpose, the details, and what matters to your audience. I help turn those pieces into a message and a visual direction that belong together.

You get a clear scope, a chance to review the work, and finished materials you can put to use. A flyer should answer the practical questions. A logo should hold together across formats. A website should help people find what they came for.

That is the standard I want every piece to meet.

Media made to move people.
