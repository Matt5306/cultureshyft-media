FLYER FACTORY GUIDE

Your Flyer Factory

A private tool on your website that turns a short brief into a print-ready flyer, social posts and an invoice. No account. Nothing to install.

FOR YOUcultureshyftmedia.com/factory/

Pick a sample, fill the brief, watch the live preview. Save a PDF, download social images, make an invoice.

FOR YOUR CLIENTScultureshyftmedia.com/order/

A client fills in what they want. Their email app opens with the request ready to send to mark@cultureshyftmedia.com.

Set it up once (10 minutes). Do this before you take money.

  1. Use your main device. Open the factory on the phone or computer you use most. Everything saves in that browser only.
  2. Add your payment details. Tap Make invoice, then Payment settings (top of the invoice screen). Enter your Cash App $cashtag, your Zelle email or phone, and your PayPal.me link. Save. Until you do, invoices show the fake placeholders $YourCashtag, you@example.com and paypal.me/YourName.
  3. Test your email. Open the order page, fill it in with your own email, tap Send to Mark, and press Send. Then check that mark@cultureshyftmedia.com really received it.
  4. Make one practice flyer and invoice. Check the payment lines on the invoice are right before a client sees one.
  5. Prices and samples are built in: $20 per flyer, $5 extras, $10 print prep, $15 rush. You cannot edit them on the page. To change them, ask Uncle Matt.

Making a flyer in 5 steps

  1. Open /factory/ and pick a sample under Start with a sample.
  2. Replace the made-up details with the real headline, date, place and contact.
  3. Add a photo if you have one. Check the live preview.
  4. Tap Save to My flyers, then Save as PDF (US Letter) or download the Instagram square, story or Facebook cover as a PNG.
  5. Tap Make invoice, check the amounts, and send it. The invoice asks for a 50% deposit and 50% on delivery.

Good to know

  • Saved in this browser. Drafts and payment settings stay on this device. A new phone, a new computer or cleared browser data starts empty.
  • Back up flyers with Export brief. Restore with Import brief. Payment settings are not exported, so keep them written down.
  • Unlisted. Not linked from the homepage and hidden from search engines. Only people with the link find it.
  • No server storage. Nothing a client types is stored on a server.
What it does not do: it does not take card payments, confirm that you were paid, or keep customer accounts. You check Cash App, Zelle or PayPal yourself before you call an invoice paid.
