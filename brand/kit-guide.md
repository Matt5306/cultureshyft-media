# How to use this kit

Keep CultureShyft Media recognisable wherever it appears. Start with the supplied files and make a copy of a template before changing it.

### Pick the right file

| File | Use it for | Find it here |
|---|---|---|
| **SVG** | Logos that stay sharp when resized. Best for design work, websites, and printers that request vector artwork. | `logo/svg/` |
| **PDF** | Printing, proofs, and documents with a fixed layout. Confirm the printer’s specifications before ordering. | `logo/pdf/` and `templates/print/` |
| **PNG** | Social posts, presentations, email, and placing a transparent logo over a background. Avoid enlarging small PNGs. | `logo/png/`, `logo/web/`, and `logo/icons/` |

Use the horizontal logo for most layouts. Choose the stacked logo for square spaces. Use the supplied avatar files for profile pictures.

### Add the logo to Canva

Open your design. Select **Uploads → Upload files**, then choose the matching SVG from `logo/svg/`. If Canva rejects it, use the matching transparent PNG from `logo/png/`. Place it on the page and resize from a corner. If your account includes Brand Kit, save the logo there for repeat use. [Canva upload guidance](https://www.canva.com/help/upload-formats-requirements/)

### Use Manrope in Canva

Select a text box, open the font menu, and search for **Manrope**. Use **ExtraBold 800** for headlines, **Bold 700** for labels, and **Medium 500** for body text.

If you need to upload the font, get Manrope from [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Manrope). In Canva, go to **Brand → your Brand Kit → Fonts → Add to category → Upload a font**. Use static TTF or OTF files and confirm the licence. Font uploads require an eligible Canva plan and role. Canva does not support variable font uploads; use its built-in Manrope if your download contains only a variable font. [Canva font guidance](https://www.canva.com/help/upload-fonts/)

### Keep the look consistent

Use Ink `#14151A` and Paper `#FFFFFF` for most of the page. Sand `#F4F3EF` gives you a softer background. Reserve Shyft Orange `#FF5A1F` for a small accent. Keep body text dark on a light background, or white on Ink. Give headlines and the logo room to breathe.

### Use the AI prompt file

Open `brand/brand-prompt.md`. Copy its contents into your AI tool before describing the job. Add the audience, format, exact text, deadline, and desired action.

Example: “Create a square event-post layout using these brand rules. Use this approved text: [text]. Leave a plain space for the supplied logo.”

Check every name, date, price, and link. Add the real logo file yourself. Use the fixed tagline **Media made to move people.** even if older notes offer alternatives.

### The 3 spelling rules

1. Keep **CultureShyft** together as 1 word. Put a space before **Media**.
2. Capitalise the **C** and **S** in normal text: **CultureShyft Media**.
3. Spell **Shyft** with a **Y**, never an **I**.

### The rule for adding the logo to any flyer

Place the supplied logo on a plain Ink or Paper block, with the matching `color-ondark` or `color-onlight` version. Keep empty space around it equal to the height of the Y’s stem. Keep the horizontal logo at least 140 pixels wide on screen or 1.2 inches in print; keep the stacked logo at least 96 pixels or 0.8 inches wide. Resize proportionally. Never stretch, rotate, redraw, retype, recolor, or add effects. Keep it off orange backgrounds and busy photos. Check readability at the final viewing size before exporting.
