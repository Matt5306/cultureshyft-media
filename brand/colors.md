# Colours

Two colours do the work. Everything else is support.

| Name | Use | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Shyft Orange** | the accent: the antenna arcs, the Y in the wordmark, buttons, one highlight per layout | `#FF5A1F` | 255, 90, 31 | 0, 65, 88, 0 |
| **Ink** | text, the mark body, dark backgrounds | `#14151A` | 20, 21, 26 | 75, 68, 60, 80 (rich black) |
| Paper | light backgrounds | `#FFFFFF` | 255, 255, 255 | 0, 0, 0, 0 |
| Sand | soft light background, cards on a page | `#F4F3EF` | 244, 243, 239 | 3, 2, 5, 0 |
| Slate | secondary text, captions, "MEDIA" | `#6A6E78` | 106, 110, 120 | 60, 48, 40, 10 |
| Ember tint | a wash behind orange elements, highlights | `#FFE8DC` | 255, 232, 220 | 0, 10, 12, 0 |

## How much of each
Roughly: 60% Paper or Ink (whichever is the background), 30% Ink or Paper (text), 10% Shyft Orange. Orange is the thing you notice, so it is used once per layout, never as a background for body text.

## Pairs that are readable (WCAG AA for normal text)
- Ink on Paper, Ink on Sand, Paper on Ink: yes.
- Slate on Paper: yes for text 14 px and up.
- **White on Shyft Orange: only for large text (24 px+) or bold 19 px+.** Not for small body text.
- Shyft Orange on Ink: large text and icons only.
- Orange on white body text: no.

## Print notes
Shyft Orange is a bright colour; on uncoated paper it prints a little duller. If a printer offers Pantone, ask for **Pantone 1655 C** as the closest match. Ink should be printed as rich black (the CMYK above), not 100% K alone, on anything larger than text.
