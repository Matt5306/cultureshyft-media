### How it works

1. **Agree the job.** Confirm the materials, price, and delivery date. Pay a 50% deposit to begin.
2. **Review the work.** Packages include 2 rounds of edits unless stated otherwise. Single flyers and monthly-plan flyers include 1 round each. A round means 1 collected list of changes within the agreed direction.
3. **Get your files.** The balance is due on delivery. Files arrive as PDF, PNG, and SVG where appropriate. Logos include all 3 formats; writing includes editable text, templates include Canva links, and websites include access and handover instructions.

Monthly plans use the same split: 50% at the start of the month and the balance at the final scheduled delivery or month-end. Extras are priced and approved before work begins.
