How to use the CultureShyft Media identity: logo, color, type, voice, templates. For printers, partners, and any AI you hand this to.

The idea

Culture is the crowd. Shyft is the pattern changing. Media is the signal that carries it.

CultureShyft Media makes digital marketing materials: flyers, social posts, campaign sets, logos, websites. The name is spelled with a Y on purpose, and the Y is the brand's mark: a bold letter whose arms hold two orange broadcast arcs, an antenna. Once people see it, they stop typing "shift".

Three things to remember

  • One word: CultureShyft. Capital C, capital S, a Y.
  • One accent color, used once per layout.
  • Short headlines, left-aligned, plenty of air.

Who it is for

Small businesses, churches and youth programs, event hosts, creators: anyone with something to announce and no designer on staff.

What a client gets

Print-ready and phone-ready files, fast, that look like they belong to a brand people already trust.

CultureShyft Media · Brand guidelines3
Logo rules

Clear space, minimum size, and the don'ts.

Clear space = height of the Y's stem, all sides

Minimum size

  • Horizontal: 1.2 in / 30 mm wide in print, 140 px on screen
  • Stacked: 0.8 in / 20 mm, 96 px
  • Mark alone: 0.25 in / 6 mm, 16 px

Backgrounds

Light or dark, plain or quiet. On a busy photo, put the logo on an Ink or Paper block. Never on orange with the color lockup: use mono white there.

Don't

stretch it
color lockup on orange
shadows or glows
  • Don't retype the wordmark in Manrope; use the files.
  • Don't recolor the arcs or the Y.
  • Don't write "Culture Shift".

How much of each

About 60% background (Paper or Ink), 30% text, 10% Shyft Orange. Orange is the thing you notice, so it appears once per layout and never behind small text.

Readable pairs

  • Ink on Paper or Sand; Paper on Ink: always.
  • White on orange: 24 pt+ or bold 19 pt+ only.
  • Orange text on white: headlines only, never body.

Type

Manrope. One family, free, built into Canva.

Shyft the room.
Headlines are short and left-aligned.

Body text stays around 65 characters a line, Medium weight, line-height 1.5. Numbers as numbers: 48 hours, 3 sizes. One size jump between levels is enough; if something needs to be louder, make it orange, not bigger.

CultureShyft Media · Brand guidelines6
Voice

Bold, warm, quick, clear.

Tagline

Media made to move people.

How we write

  • Short sentences. One idea each.
  • Lead with what the reader gets.
  • Numbers as numbers.
  • No exclamation marks in body text; the orange does the shouting.
  • We say "flyer", not "collateral". "You", not "clients".
